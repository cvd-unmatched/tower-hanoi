Rules:

  • Only one disk can be moved at a time
  • A larger disk (higher number) cannot be placed on top of a smaller disk (lower number)

  • Random Mode: Stack all disks on the highlighted target tower in order (1 on top, 5 on bottom)
  • Each disk is numbered for easy identification
  • Mobile: Tap disks to select, then tap towers to place them
